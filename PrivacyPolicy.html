Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 12, 2026

1. Introduction

Thank you for using PageManager (the "Service"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your information, as well as your rights regarding your data. The Service is an automation tool designed to help you manage your Facebook Page, including publishing content and responding to interactions.

2. Information We Collect

To provide the Service, we may collect the following information:

  • Facebook Page Information: Your authorized Page ID, published content, comments, and messages data.
  • Access Tokens: The tokens you generate for Meta Graph API access, used solely to verify your identity and execute your requested actions.
  • Log Data: Basic service logs, such as request timestamps and IP addresses (without personal identifiers), used for troubleshooting and service stability.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information only for the following purposes:

  • Execute automated actions you request, such as posting content and replying to comments/messages.
  • Verify your identity and ensure only authorized users can access your Page data.
  • Maintain, optimize, and troubleshoot the Service.

We will not sell, trade, or disclose your personal or Page data to third parties without your explicit consent, unless required by law.

4. Data Storage and Security

  • Your access tokens and Page data are processed only on your local or self-hosted server; we do not permanently store sensitive tokens.
  • We use industry-standard security measures, including HTTPS encryption, to protect your data during transmission.
  • You may revoke access or reset your access tokens at any time via the Facebook Developers Platform.

5. Your Rights

Under applicable data protection laws, you have the right to:

  • Access: Review the scope of data we access related to your Page.
  • Correction: Request correction of any inaccurate data.
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your data or revoke authorization.
  • Withdraw Consent: Stop the Service from collecting your data at any time.

6. Third-Party Services

The Service uses Meta Platforms, Inc.’s Graph API and Webhook services. Your use is also governed by the Meta Privacy Policy.

7. Children's Privacy

The Service is not intended for users under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe we have collected such data, please contact us to have it removed.
